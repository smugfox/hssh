# Seed directions (Discover step 2)

Finding: all four runs converged on a carbon-copy paper form. Shared: Redaction or a slab headline, ultramarine ink, OCR or mono fields, flatbed halftone hardware, a stamp with no bounce, Decline as the primary action with a full receipt. The brief likely primed this by asking what a request and receipt look like as objects.

## Yellow Copy
Customer's copy of a triplicate form. Page ground #EDE31E, ink #3415AF, slip #F4F1E2, Decline oxblood #8D033D, Allow green #5EE44E, pink stub #F76862. Redaction / Public Sans / Martian Mono. 34:15 form with a "for your records" margin. Stamp lands in 120ms, receipt tears down the perforation. Decline is larger and holds default focus.

## Carbon Copy (blue on flimsy)
Government form written by someone on your side. Flimsy #F6F1DF, carbon blue #2415F4, plum #5E0445, vermilion #E2563D, lime #9DC205 on "Live" only. Redaction / Public Sans / OCR-B. 2:4:1:5 columns, receipt column is a dotted outline until you answer. Declined fields degrade into Redaction 100, stamped "Kept."

## Ditto
Bank slip at a counter. Mint #8BEB97, slip #EFFAF7, ink #24038A, violet #542C8F, stamp red #CA344C, highlighter #EFFA16. Besley Black / Libre Franklin / OCR-B. Slips split 4:7 with drawn perforation, engraved line drawings, faint guilloche. Stub tears off and pins to the margin as your receipt, following you down the page.

## Carbon Copy (county clerk)
Clerk's counter. Ink #181AE1, top sheet #F2F7F3, sage #97BBA1, decline #EED4D6, rose #C87079, highlighter #ACED46. Redaction / OCR-B / Public Sans. 44px rule as the row and tap target. Top sheet tears to reveal your copy. Decline yields a pink copy reading "Nothing left."
