# Direction: Yellow Copy

The customer's copy of a form, and it's yours to keep. Dry, exact, a little loud. Concept B (the handshake) with A's clarity: the hero request is Agent One asking for something in service of your morning brief.

**Palette:** page #EDE31E, ink #3415AF for all text and rules, slip paper #F4F1E2, stamp oxblood #8D033D. Allow stamps in ink blue. Green and pink from the seed direction are cut.

**Type:** Redaction for headlines and reading text (degraded cut for the stamp), Martian Mono for labels, typed values and controls. Square corners, no shadows, no gradients.

**Grid:** one long form on a 34:15 split. Wide column is numbered boxes, narrow column is a "for your records" margin holding the plain caveats. Rows sit on hairline rules.

**The request slip:** same fields everywhere: Requester, Wants, Scope, Duration. Hero, each product and the consent steps all use it.

**The stub:** a numbered panel on the slip, flat, there from the start. Answering stamps it. Your copy number then follows you in the header. Allow or Decline both produce one.

**Allow and Decline:** equal weight, Decline first. Allow reveals the brief item. Decline leaves that line of the brief blanked out and the stub reads "Nothing left." Page continues the same either way.

**Images:** no photography, no mascot. Puppy One is a technical line drawing with callouts.

**Motion:** nothing moves until you answer. Stamp lands in 120ms with no bounce, straight, ink 2px out of register. Reduced motion cuts to the stamped state.

**Avoid:** anything that looks like a real government form: no eagle seal, no "Department of", no agency names. It is a hussh form.

**Rule-breaking choice:** the whole landing page is a single form you are filling in, and the last section is the status ledger.

All copy is draft.

- Round 2: 6/10. Sections still too similar, tilted stub reads as a cliche, laptop halftone looks like a filter, manifesto underscaled, inconsistent vertical rhythm.
- Round 3: 6/10. Three hero-scale headlines compete, tilted stub plus perforation plus stamp stack paper tropes, laptop image is weak, vertical rhythm has no system, closing CTA bar is generic.

## Deliver check
